EDITORIAL FOREWORD

Where Memories Become Timeless Art

Welcome to the premiere edition of the DecorSpot Studio Journal. Photography, at its highest expression, is the quiet preservation of raw emotion and intimate milestones. Located opposite HM College in Randar, Muvattupuzha, Kerala, our boutique studio was created as a sanctuary where families and brands can freeze time.

In this edition, we invite you behind the lens to explore our four flagship portfolios: from our hospital-grade, climate-controlled baby suite to ethereal outdoor maternity sessions, whimsical birthday celebrations, and high-strobe commercial fashion campaigns.

“We don’t just take pictures; we craft heirlooms that your family will treasure across generations.”
